About our school

Triple A Legacy International School provides learning opportunities for children at every stage of their education.

Who we are

We are an educational institution that supports children from creche to college. Our aim is to help every pupil make real progress and to recognise their achievements along the way.

Our colourful emblem reflects the bright, varied talents found in every classroom.

Our motto

Learning today for a better tomorrow.

Education prepares pupils for a brighter future. We value learning, personal development and academic progress.

What we stand for

Progress

We track and celebrate each pupil's growth through the academic year.

Achievement

Attainment is recognised, from classroom milestones to graduation.

Development

We support the whole child, not only exam results.

Frequently asked questions

Which age groups do you teach?

We run Creche, Early Years, Nursery, Primary, an After School Club and College.

What is the Primary Graduation Certificate?

It is awarded to pupils for their progress and attainment during the academic year, and is signed and dated by the Head Teacher.

How do I apply?

Use our contact page to send an admission enquiry. We will reply with the next steps.
