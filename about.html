About our school

Triple A Legacy International School provides learning opportunities for children at every stage of their education.

Who we are

We are an educational institution supporting children from creche to college. Our aim is to help every pupil make real progress and to recognise their achievements along the way.

Our colourful emblem, a rainbow arc with a bold tick and two shining stars, reflects the bright, varied talents in every classroom.

Our motto

Learning today for a better tomorrow.

Education prepares pupils for a brighter future. We value learning, personal development and academic progress.

Frequently asked questions

Which age groups do you teach?

We run Creche, Early Years, Nursery, Primary, an After School Club and College (JSS 1-3).

What is the Primary Graduation Certificate?

It is awarded for a pupil's progress and attainment during the academic year, and is signed and dated by the Head Teacher.

Is the school government approved?

Yes, Triple A Legacy International School is government approved.
